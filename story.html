The Beginning

About Us

Butt Karahi began in Lahore in 1975, built on one idea: that a karahi cooked fresh, with quality halal meat and the right balance of spices, is worth doing properly. Four decades later, the Butt family recipes are still the heart of every dish we serve.

From the historic food streets of Lakshmi Chowk in Lahore to the bustling dining halls of Mississauga and Pickering, we never take shortcuts. Every meal is a tribute to the culinary culture of Punjab.

Crossing Continents

Our Journey

That tradition crossed continents with Imtiaz Butt, founder and head chef of Butt Karahi Canada. He still leads the kitchen across all three of our locations in Mississauga and Pickering, holding every plate to the same standard: the finest fresh meat, cut and spiced to order, no shortcuts.

Goat, lamb, veal and chicken karahis simmered to order; naan comes hot from the tandoor; and seekh kebabs go on the grill only after you order them. Good food takes a little time, and we think it's worth the wait.
